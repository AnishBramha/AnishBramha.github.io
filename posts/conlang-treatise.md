### Background

My conlang Granthōmukhīm (_lit._ 'the literary–faced one') has been in the works ever since I knew conlanging was a thing. This is the first edition of a grammar treatise, covering the language all the way from the basics to the deepest nuances, from the phonology to building your own lexicon. The vocabulary of the language is still in development, and an exhaustive list of root words will be compiled in the near future.

*Edit:* (**August 2026**) The second edition incorporates a new system of compounding, an expanded vocabulary for colours derived from elements of nature, and a root word glossary at the end, which is still in development.
